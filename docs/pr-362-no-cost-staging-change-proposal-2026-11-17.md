## Stop and recovery procedure

- **Before the first committed migration:** abort; there is no database change to undo. Preserve the fresh checkpoint and preflight evidence.
- **During a guarded correction or the post-head transaction:** allow the transaction to fail/roll back, then read back history and catalog to prove no partial effect. Do not retry against a changed before-state without a new review.
- **After any successful migration file:** stop workers and new writes, leave outward switches off, record the exact last applied version and errors, and make a fresh *post-failure* logical dump before repair. Compare it with the pre-change checkpoint. Prefer a separately reviewed forward repair from the known partial state. Do **not** run `supabase db reset --linked`, replay the canonical 259/260, rewrite migration history, or run an untested reverse script.
- **If forward repair cannot preserve staging data:** the pre-change logical checkpoint is the recovery source, including roles, Auth data, history, 14 initial Storage policies, trigger, buckets and any newly captured Storage bytes. Rebuilding **within the existing hosted project** would require a separately reviewed destructive cleanup/restore sequence and a maintenance outage; no such hosted restore has been verified. Obtain approval for that exact recovery action after inspecting the failure state. Do not assert that the local restore proves this path.

**Approval boundary:** approval of this proposal would authorize only the scoped in-place staging sequence after the fresh gates pass. It would also explicitly accept that complete hosted rollback is unproven and recovery after committed migrations may require a separately approved rebuild or forward repair. It would not authorize production, paid resources, provider publication, history relabeling or Phase 8/9 acceptance.

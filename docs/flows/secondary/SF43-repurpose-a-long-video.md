# SF43 — Repurpose a long video into clips

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Content Production Factory → Edit / Repurpose
**Owner.** Creative lead. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF08.

## Why it matters commercially
One shoot legitimately becomes a month of content. For a service business where the client
can only be filmed occasionally, this is the difference between a feed that runs dry and one
that does not.

## Do it
1. **Repurpose video** → **Video to repurpose** → **Repurpose direction** — say what angle
   the clips should take.
2. The repurpose insights agent reads the source and proposes clips.
3. Clip extraction runs as its own job, producing derivatives.
4. Each derivative keeps a link to its source video.

## Lineage is the point
A derivative carries its source, so:
- Rights on the source apply to every clip (SF25) — if the customer in the footage withdraws
  permission, every clip is affected
- Attribution can trace a lead back through the clip to the original shoot (SF77)

## Gotcha
Repurposing does not re-brief. A clip inherits the source's brief, so a clip that needs a
different claim or a different call to action needs its own copy (SF40) — and if the claim
changes, its own proof.

## Re-entry
A repurposed clip can go back through briefing if it needs to become its own piece properly.

# SF28 — Sign off a proof artifact version

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Proof Production Factory → Proof Artifacts
**Owner.** Client manager. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF06.

## The one rule
**The build is the agent's; the signoff is a person's.** An artifact that was generated has
not been approved by anybody, and nothing can be placed from an unsigned version.

## Do it
1. Read the artifact in full.
2. Check it against the source proof: does every claim in the artifact actually appear in the
   evidence? An artifact that rounds "saved about a fifth" up to "saves 25%" has invented a
   quantified claim.
3. Check the source's rights are `approved` and not expiring soon (SF25).
4. Sign off.

## What a signoff binds
The exact version. Rebuild the artifact and the new version needs its own signoff; the
placement keeps pointing at the approved one until you place the new one.

# SF44 — Hand a video to a human editor and take delivery

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Content Production Factory → Briefs →
**Team member** / **View delivery**
**Owner.** Creative lead. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF08.

## Do it
1. Brief it properly first (SF34). The brief is what the person works against and what QA
   later judges the result by.
2. Set **Format** and **Destination** before dispatch — a story and a reel are different jobs.
3. **Team member** — dispatch to someone under **Team → Editors** (or Avatars for on-camera).
4. **View delivery** to see what came back.

## The assignment lifecycle
`assigned` → `accepted` → `delivered` → `approved`, with `declined`, `rework` and `cancelled`.

**Rework is a state, not a failure.** Send it back naming the specific brief clause it missed,
not "it doesn't feel right".

## Record what the library needs
On delivery: **Video title**, **Video source**, **Video format**, **Usage rights**.

**Usage rights matter as much here as on proof.** Footage of a customer carries that
customer's permission; treat it as `not_cleared` until the permission is recorded (SF25).

## Then
QA (SF41), the video acceptance screen, and a person's approval (SF42). A human-made asset
still cannot go out on QA alone.

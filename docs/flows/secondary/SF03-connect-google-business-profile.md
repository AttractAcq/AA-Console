# SF03 — Connect a Google Business Profile location

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Google Profile → Location
**Owner.** Client manager. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF01, MF06.

## Why it matters
A prospect will search the business before they buy. The profile is exit track X-f —
omnipresence — and it is also where real reviews come from for the Proof Bank (SF26).

## Do it
1. **Connect a Business Profile location** → **Connect a location**.
2. Enter the location and **Address** as Google holds it.
3. **Connect.**

The panel then reads *"Connected. Nothing is read until a credential exists."* That sentence
is literal: connecting records which location this client is, and nothing is read or written
until the credential is in place.

## What it unlocks
- **Reviews** — read them, draft replies (SF26), capture them as proof
- **Posts** — publish approved proof posts (SF56)
- **Metrics** — what the profile actually did
- `google_business_profile` as a supported proof destination (SF29)

## Gotcha
Profile *management* — hours, description, photos — is record-only. Changes are recorded and a
person makes them in Google. That is gap **G05**.

## Troubleshooting
Read `google_profile_due`. **Gone but cited** means a review was deleted but is still cited
somewhere; find the citation and retract it (SF32).

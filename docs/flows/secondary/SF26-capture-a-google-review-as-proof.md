# SF26 — Capture a Google review as proof, and reply to it

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Google Profile → Reviews
**Owner.** Client manager. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF06.

## Capture it
**Capture as proof** pulls a real review into the Proof Bank. It arrives
*"Captured into the Proof Bank, uncleared"* and the row then reads **Captured as proof**.

**Capture and clearance are separate acts on purpose.** A public review is evidence that
exists; whether the client may quote it in an ad is a different question (SF25).

## Reply to it
1. **Draft a reply** → *"Drafted. It waits for a person."*
2. Read the draft. Change it — a reply in the client's voice beats a correct one in nobody's.
3. **Approve this reply** → *"Approved. It posts when the adapter runs."*

The draft is the agent's; the reply is a person's. Replying to reviews is part of
omnipresence: a prospect reads the replies as closely as the reviews.

## Watch for: Gone but cited
`google_profile_due` flags **Gone but cited** — a review that has been deleted but is still
cited somewhere. That is the expensive case, because the citation is now unevidenced. Find
the citation and retract it (SF32).

## Gotcha
A negative review is also proof — of how the client handles problems. Reply to it properly
rather than hiding it; do not capture it as a positive claim.

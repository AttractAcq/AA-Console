# SF57 — Build a page, audit it, revise it

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Website Studio → Landing Pages / Offer Pages
**Owner.** Client manager. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF11, MF14, MF15.

## Do it
1. **Brief the page** — with **Anything to steer it (optional)** for the angle. The brief
   reads the campaign, the offer version and the ICP.
2. **Build Page** queues the landing page agent.
3. **Audit** it — the page auditor finds the problems.
4. **Revise** it — the page reviser fixes them.
5. Iterate here, **before** publishing. Revising a published page means a new approval and a
   new probe (MF11).

## Page kinds
`page_type` is `landing`, `offer` or `recruitment`. Primary landing pages and secondary offer
pages are different things with different jobs; recruitment pages belong to AA's house client
and are deliberately kept out of client lists (MF14).

## What to check yourself
- The offer figures match the **current signed-off** offer version (SF22)
- Every claim has proof behind it (SF21)
- **The form exists and points at a real capture endpoint** (SF61) — the auditor checks the
  page, not whether the endpoint works
- It reads in the client's voice

## Gotcha
A built page is not published, and a published page is not working. Those are three different
states and MF11 walks all three.

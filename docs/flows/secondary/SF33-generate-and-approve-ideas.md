# SF33 — Generate ideas for a campaign, and approve or reject one

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Content Production Factory → Ideation
**Owner.** Creative lead. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF03, MF07, MF08, MF09.

## Do it
Four kinds, and the choice matters:

| Button | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Auto Idea** | The ideation agent proposes from intelligence and pillars |
| **Pillar Idea** | Against a specific content pillar (SF09) |
| **Proof Idea** | Rooted in a specific proof asset — **the one that converts** |
| **Manual Idea** → **Add idea** | Yours, typed in |

- **Assign to campaign (optional)** — set it, or the asset will not count toward a campaign's
  `creative` manifest item.
- For a batch: **Choose between 1 and 60 ideas.**

Then per idea: **Approve**, **Approve & brief** (approves and queues the brief in one step),
or **Archive**.

## States
`draft` → `approved` → `briefed`, or `rejected`.

## Archive the bad ones
Rejecting is part of the job. An archived idea stops the engine re-proposing it, so a few
minutes archiving now saves seeing the same weak idea every week.

## Gotcha
**Proof Idea** is the highest-converting kind and the one that gets skipped because it needs
proof to exist first. That is the real argument for starting SF24 on day one.

# SF24 — Collect proof from a customer with a capture link

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Proof Production Factory → Proof Bank →
**Prepare evidence request** → **Capture link**
**Owner.** Client manager. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF06.

## Why this is the most valuable thing in MF06
A customer's own words and files are the strongest proof there is, and the slowest to get.
**Start this on day one of an engagement** — it is the long-lead item, and campaigns stall
waiting for it.

## Do it
1. **Prepare evidence request** — what you are asking the customer for. Be specific: "photos
   before and after, and what the job cost" beats "a testimonial".
2. **Capture link** — generates the link the customer uses.
3. The client sends it to a real customer. The client asking is far more effective than us
   asking.
4. The customer submits. The submission arrives for review.
5. Review it, then record rights and expiry (SF25) — a submission is not automatically
   cleared.

## Gotcha
The submission is the customer's, and the consent they gave is part of the record. Do not
quietly broaden the use beyond what the request asked for — if the request said "on your
website" and you want it in an ad, ask again.

## Then
SF27 to turn it into placeable artifacts.

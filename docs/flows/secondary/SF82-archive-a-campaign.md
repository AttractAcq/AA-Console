# SF82 — Archive a campaign without orphaning external objects

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Archive → Campaigns
**Owner.** Marketing Director. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF16.

## The one rule
**Archiving in the console does not pause anything in Meta.** An archived campaign whose ads
are still live is the worst possible state: spending real money, and invisible on the board
because somebody tidied up.

## Before you archive
1. **MF13 is done.** Once it is off the board, nobody reports on it. Read the result and move
   the opportunity's confidence first (SF15).
2. **Paid is paused in Ads Manager, by a person.** The console cannot do this (**G07**).
3. **Pages it owned are accounted for.** A published page outlives its campaign. Decide
   whether it stays up — if it does, it must stay in MF15's drift sweep.
4. **Magnets it owned are accounted for.** An approved magnet version keeps delivering.
   Revoke it if it should stop (SF63).
5. **Capture endpoints are revoked** if nothing should keep creating leads (SF61).
6. **Sales agent deployments are disabled** if the agent was campaign-specific (SF67).
7. **Check what you are starving.** If another campaign feeds from this one — the P1 → R1
   relationship — pausing this one degrades that one over the following fortnight.

## Do it
Archive the campaign. It becomes readable, not deleted:
**Archive** keeps **Content Production**, **Pages**, **Campaigns** and **Sales Agents**.

## Also archive the ideas
If the campaign's ideas were wrong, archive them too (SF33), or the engine proposes them
again next week.

## Ending a whole engagement
Turn off all five switches first (SF06). That stops everything external at once, and is the
single most important step. Then work through the list above per campaign.

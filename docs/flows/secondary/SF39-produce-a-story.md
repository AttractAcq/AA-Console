# SF39 — Produce a story

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Content Production Factory → Create / **Stories**
**Owner.** SMM. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF07, MF09.

## Do it
1. Brief with **Format** = `story` (SF34). **A story is vertical** — the format drives the
   aspect, so setting it wrong produces an unusable asset.
2. Build, QA (SF41), approve (SF42).
3. Schedule it (SF49). Stories are time-sensitive: a story scheduled for next week is
   usually a story not worth posting.

## What stories are for in this delivery
Stories carry the timed plays in the convert layer: the deadline, the "last two slots", the
behind-the-scenes that makes a follower feel current. They are the lowest-friction way to
keep an S2 audience warm between posts.

## Gotcha
Stories expire on the platform. The asset stays in the library and the post record stays, but
the public artifact is gone within a day — so do not place proof whose only home is a story
(MF06), and do not point an ad at one.

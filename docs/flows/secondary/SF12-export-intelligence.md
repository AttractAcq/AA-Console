# SF12 — Export intelligence as PDF or ZIP

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Intelligence OS
**Owner.** Client manager. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026.

## Do it
The export control on the module exports either:

- **One tab as a PDF** — the active tab's record.
- **The whole module as a ZIP** — every tab in the current view.

The view matters: `?view=intelligence` exports the intelligence tabs, `?view=strategy`
exports the strategy tabs. They are separate exports.

## What it is for
Client-facing deliverables at the start of an engagement, and a record of what the campaign
was built on. Pair it with SF11 — an export is a readable version of roughly what the
snapshot froze.

## Gotcha
Export what a person has read and corrected, not raw agent output. An export is the most
client-facing artifact in Intelligence OS, and it will be read closely.

# SF75 — Generate reporting commentary

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Attribution OS → Commentary
**Owner.** Marketing Director. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF13.

## Do it
**Reporting Commentary** generates the written read from the figures. Then **read and edit
it**.

## What the agent cannot know
- That the client paused their phones for a week
- That a competitor opened nearby
- That coverage is partial (SF74) — it sees the numbers, not the gaps
- That a sale recorded this week actually closed last month

All of those change the meaning of the same figures. Add them.

## The rule for editing
**Never let commentary launder an estimate into a fact.** If coverage is partial, the
commentary must say so in the client-facing text, not in a footnote nobody reads.

Equally: do not let it read a publication as a commercial result. "We published 14 pieces" is
activity; "we produced 9 leads at £22" is a result.

## What good commentary does
Says what happened, what it means, what we are changing, and what we do not yet know. The
last of those is what makes the first three believable.

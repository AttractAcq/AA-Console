# SF08 — Write business context the agents can use

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Intelligence OS → Business Context
**Owner.** Client manager. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF01.

## Do it
1. **Business Input** — the form the agent reads, including
   **What you know from talking to them (optional)**. Use that field; it is where the things
   that are not on the website go.
2. **Draft the business context.**
3. Read the result and **Edit** it.

## What makes it usable
- **Specific services, not categories.** "Garage conversions and loft conversions in
  Hampshire" beats "building services".
- **Who they lose to and why.** Competitors by name, and the reason.
- **Constraints.** Areas they will not travel to, jobs they will not take, a minimum value.
  These stop campaigns targeting work the client cannot service.
- **The client's own phrases.** The copywriter and the sales agent both pick these up.

## Gotcha
A thin business context fails quietly. The agents still produce confident, well-formed output
— it is just generic, and you find out when the ads do not convert. If you cannot fill this
properly, get back on a call with the client before running anything else.

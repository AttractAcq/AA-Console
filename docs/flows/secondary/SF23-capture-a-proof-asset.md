# SF23 — Capture a proof asset by hand

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Proof Production Factory → Proof Bank
**Owner.** Client manager. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF06.

## Do it
Record, at minimum:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Proof type** | `customer_result`, `testimonial`, `review`, `case_study`, `before_after`, `stat`, `credential`, `award`, `press`, `process`, `team_expertise` |
| **Media type** | `image`, `text` or `video` — **required** |
| **Source** | Where it came from. A URL, a person, a document |
| **Evidence** | What backs it up |
| Title | What it is, readably |

Then set rights and expiry (SF25). It arrives `not_cleared`.

## Pick the type honestly
`process` and `team_expertise` are easy to produce and convert least. `customer_result`,
`testimonial` and `review` are hard to get and convert most. A Proof Bank full of `process`
is a Proof Bank that will not close anybody — which is why SF24 should start on day one.

## The source is not optional in spirit
A proof asset with no source cannot be defended if the claim is challenged, and it will fail
the artifact and claim checks later. If you do not know where it came from, find out before
recording it.

## Other ways in
- **Find proof online** — the proof finder searches for what exists publicly. It proposes; it
  never clears.
- A customer submission (SF24).
- A Google review (SF26).

# SF16 — Build the awareness journey

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Market Opportunities → Strategize →
**Awareness journey**
**Owner.** Marketing Director. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF02, MF10.

## Why it matters
`opportunity_readiness` refuses to call an opportunity ready without it:
*"No awareness journey, so there is nothing to brief content or proof from."*

Every brief in MF07 and MF08 points at one of these stages. One ad answers one stage.

## Do it
For each awareness stage of this niche, record five things:

| Field | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Stage** | The awareness level, in your own words |
| **Mindset** | What they believe and feel at that point |
| **Message** | The one thing to say to move them one step |
| **Asset direction** | What the asset should be and do |
| **Distribution note** | Where this lands — profile, page, ad, post |

## The judgement call nobody else can make for you — G06
Nothing in the system maps an awareness stage to one of the sixteen templates. Make the call
and **write the template you intend into the distribution note**, so whoever builds the
campaign does not guess.

Rough guide:

| Mindset | Likely template |
|---|---|
| Does not know the problem has a name | P4, P1 |
| Knows the problem, not the solutions | P1, P5 |
| Comparing solutions | R1, P5 |
| Comparing providers | R1, O1 |
| Ready, needs a reason now | O1 |
| Nearly bought and stopped | R3 |
| Objecting | R2 |

## Gotcha
Write fewer, truer stages rather than a complete textbook ladder. Three stages you can
actually write ads for beat five you cannot.

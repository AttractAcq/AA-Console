# SF48 — Terminate or archive a piece of content

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Content Production Factory, or
Archive → Content Production
**Owner.** Client manager. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF16.

## When to terminate
- The proof behind it is gone or its rights lapsed (SF25, SF32)
- The claim became untrue — a price changed, a guarantee ended (MF15)
- The client asked
- It is simply wrong and should not be re-proposed

## Do it
Terminate or archive the record. A terminated piece stops being scheduled and stops being
re-proposed by the engine.

Archive keeps it readable: **Archive → Content Production**, alongside **Pages**,
**Campaigns** and **Sales Agents**. Nothing is deleted.

## The gotcha that matters
**Terminating the record does not unpublish the post.** If the content is already live:
1. Remove it on the platform too.
2. If it cited proof whose rights were lost, treat it as a retraction and check the other
   surfaces (SF32).

The console records; the platform publishes. Only one of those is under our control after the
fact.

## Also archive the idea
If the piece was wrong because the idea was wrong, archive the idea too (SF33) — otherwise
the engine proposes it again next week.

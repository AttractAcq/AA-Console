# SF14 — Score an opportunity and record the factors

**Where.** Clients → [client] → Delivery → Market Opportunities → Evaluate
**Owner.** Marketing Director. **Reviewed.** 25 October 2026. **Part of** MF02.

## Do it
1. Score the opportunity. The panel shows the score with its **Band**.
2. **Record the factors, not just the total.** The factors are what a reviewer argues with;
   a bare number is unarguable and therefore useless.
3. Fill the commercial fields: **Desired outcome**, purchase trigger,
   **Conversion action**, positioning. The campaign brief and the offer both read these.

## Why the factors matter more than the score
Two niches can score the same for opposite reasons — one has high demand and brutal
competition, the other low demand and nobody serving it. The strategy for each is different,
and only the factors tell you which you are looking at.

## Reject the ones that fail
A rejected opportunity with a reason is more useful than a long list of maybes. The reason
stops it being re-proposed and re-argued next quarter.

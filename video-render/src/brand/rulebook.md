# Motion grammar

This rulebook is read by the storyboard writer and the independent visual critic. The renderer enforces the same limits; prose alone is not a gate.

- One idea per scene. Each scene renders one approved library component.
- Each on-screen text block has at most seven words. Captions have at most two lines and 32 characters per line while narration plays.
- Every number animates into view. Lists reveal one item at a time in sync with its narration.
- A text block remains visible at least 0.18 seconds per word and at least 1.2 seconds overall.
- The caption layer owns the lower safe area and never overlaps a component's text region.
- Use only `cut`, `fade`, `slideLeft`, `slideUp`, or `wipeBrand`. Cut between sibling bullets; fade between sections; wipe only into or out of a title card.
- A scene lasts from 2 to 25 seconds. Split a longer thought into separate scenes.
- Establish the hook within eight seconds. Recap and Outro are the final two scenes.
- The scene plan names colour roles. It never contains a literal hex colour.
- Use typography, vector shapes, licensed SVG icons and data graphics. No footage, stock image, avatar or raster background appears in generated scenes.

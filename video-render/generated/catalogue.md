# AA video component catalogue

## TitleCard

Opens a video or a major section with one clear promise. Avoid dense explanations and unverified outcome claims. Title limit: 60 characters.

## SectionCard

Introduces one numbered chapter and emits its chapter marker. Use between ideas, not between every sentence. At most nine chapters.

## KineticHeadline

A short spoken claim revealed one word at a time. Use for a central takeaway, not a paragraph or unsupported performance claim. Twelve words maximum.

## BulletReveal

Explains two to six short supporting points with a paced reveal. Avoid long paragraphs and use supplied alignment times when narration must match an item.

## StepFlow

Shows two to six ordered steps. It stacks vertically on portrait output and runs horizontally on landscape output. Use when order matters, not for unrelated bullets.

## Compare

Compares two choices on equal terms, with up to four short points each. It stacks on portrait output. Do not use when the evidence for either side is incomplete.

## Timeline

Shows two to seven milestones along one ordered path. The at label can be a date or phase name; the author must confirm chronology before use.

## ProcessDiagram

Shows a small directed process using named nodes and edges. The layout is deterministic and has no author-supplied coordinates. Avoid cyclic or densely connected graphs.

## BigNumber

Emphasizes one sourced numerical fact with an animated count and short label. Supply only a verified value; the component does not validate its provenance.

## Chart

Shows a small sourced data comparison as a bar, line, or pie chart. Use two to eight categories and no more than three series; label units in narration or adjacent copy.

## Equation

Presents a short mathematical relationship rendered by KaTeX. Verify the formula and define each symbol in narration; avoid equations that need a full proof.

## CodeBlock

Explains a short code example with groups of lines revealed in order. Use only when code is central to the explanation; keep within 600 characters.

## Quote

Displays a brief attributed quotation. Quote wording and permission must be checked against its source before publication.

## Outro

Ends the video with one next action. Always use as the final scene; do not add multiple competing calls to action.
